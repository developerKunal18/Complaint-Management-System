1. Features
- Register and view complainants
- Submit complaints with category and priority
- Generate unique complaint IDs
- Assign complaints to staff members
- Track complaint status and resolution notes
- Add comments and updates
- Prevent invalid status transitions
- Search complaints by ID or status
- Generate complaint summary reports
- Store data in JSON files
